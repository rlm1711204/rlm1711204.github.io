      Google Drive backup

      How do I get a Client ID? (free, 10 minutes, one time)
      1. Open console.cloud.google.com and sign in with your Gmail.
      2. Top bar → Select a project → New project → name it Kaasu → Create.
      3. Search bar → Google Drive API → Enable.
      4. Menu → Google Auth Platform (or "OAuth consent screen") → Get started. App name Kaasu, your email, Audience External → Create.
      5. Audience → Test users → Add users → add your own Gmail → Save.
      6. Clients (or "Credentials") → Create client → type Web application.
      7. Under Authorised JavaScript origins add: → Create.
      8. For iPhone: also under Authorised redirect URIs add:
      9. Copy the Client ID (ends with .apps.googleusercontent.com) and paste it above.

      When Google says "Google hasn't verified this app", tap Continue — it's your own app.

      🤖 AI bill reading (optional)

      With a free AI key, Kaasu reads bill photos much more accurately and can read PDF bills and statements (PhonePe, Paytm, bank) — every transaction at once. Keys stay on this phone only; they're never saved to Google Drive or backups.

      Google Gemini keys (free)
        How do I get a free Gemini key? (2 minutes, no card)
        1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with your Gmail.
        2. Tap Create API key (accept the terms if asked) and copy it.
        3. Paste it above and tap Add. You can add a key from a second Gmail too — when one reaches its free daily limit, Kaasu uses the next.
        Claude key (optional, paid)
        Used only if Gemini can't read a file. Get one at console.anthropic.com.

        Typing tips

        • Spent: chai 20 · petrol 500 upi · rent 12,000 on 5th
        • Income: salary 55000 · bonus 10k · cashback 50
        • Saved: sip 5000 · fd 1 lakh · gold 3000 · ppf 2000
        • Gave: gave ravi 2000 · lent 500 to anu
        • Took: took 500 from kumar · borrowed 2k from anand
        • Returned: ravi returned 1000 · paid back kumar 500
        • Dates: yesterday · 2 days ago · last monday · 5/9 · 5 sep
        • Bills & statements: tap the scan button → 📷 photo or 📄 PDF (PDFs need a free Gemini key)
        • Many at once: chai 20, auto 60, milk 30
        • Given, not a loan: gave manoj 500 gift · paid for dilmaan 300 no return
        • Budget head: add need or want, e.g. shopping 800 need
        • Every month: rent 12000 every month on 5th · salary 55000 monthly on 1st
